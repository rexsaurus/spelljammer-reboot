## Overall arc
1. Earn money with trade and passenger runs.
2. Gear up the crew, capture or upgrade to a stronger ship.
3. Do the main quest.

## Main-quest steps (as we read them)
| # | Step | Trigger / where |
|---|---|---|
| A | A Waterdeep envoy visits you at a port and gives you the Neogi quest | Happens once the party is high enough level (the guide suggests around 7) |
| B | During normal play, you meet a ship carrying a messenger from Clive; hail it | Random space encounter |
| C | Visit Clive at Clive's Den (Garden). He marks an abandoned dwarven citadel | The citadel shows up under "other points of interest" |
| D | Board the citadel and clear the undead (lich included) to get a disguise sceptre | Boarding battle |
| E | Go back to Clive. He marks a Neogi gathering | New point of interest |
| F | Hail the gathering (the sceptre is used automatically). You're sent to a raid rendezvous | New point of interest |
| G | At the raid, the Neogi captain gives up the hidden mothership's location | New point of interest |
| H | Fight a chain of battles: several deathspiders, a mindspider, then the mothership | Ship combat |
| I | Destroying the mothership plays the ending cinematic and credits | Play can continue afterward |

Rules notes: ship movement is effectively 2D (plane-bound). Weapons can target Hull or Weapons, so hit-and-run against
weapons works on big hulls.

## Rules notes from the manual (paraphrased)
- A ship's starting hull points equal its tonnage. Tonnage also limits crew and cargo.
- Ship's rating (speed) depends on the helm and the helmsman's level. Maneuverability class comes from the hull design.
- Ramming needs ships of roughly similar tonnage (a ram is required).
